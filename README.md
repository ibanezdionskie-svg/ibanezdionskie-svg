## Featured projects

### 🥫 Scanity: Personalized Food-Safety Scanner
**[Live demo](https://scanity-eta.vercel.app)** · [Repository](https://github.com/Alvin187187/Scanity) · *Team project · Full-stack developer*

Food labels are hard to read, especially for people with allergies or health conditions. Scanity checks a packaged product against the user's personal health profile and explains the result in plain language.

- Onboarding that records allergies (e.g., shellfish, tree nuts, lactose intolerance) and health conditions (e.g., hypertension, kidney disease, celiac disease)
- Barcode and OCR label scanning, with a personal health rating and allergy alerts for each product
- Plain-language ingredient explanations, product comparison, and scan history
- Complete account flow: register, log in, reset password, settings, language, and account deletion

**My contribution:** Full-stack developer. I built front-end screens, including the barcode and OCR scanning flows, product comparison, and the About screen, and worked on the back end.

`React 19` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `Vite` `Python` `FastAPI` `Supabase` `Vercel`


### 💰 TrackOn: Household Expense Tracker
**[Live demo](https://track0n-hkor.vercel.app)** · [Repository](https://github.com/ibanezdionskie-svg/track0n)

Small purchases add up quickly, and most households don't know where their money goes each month. TrackOn helps a household record everyday spending, organize it by category, and compare it against a budget.

- **Receipt scanning** turns a photo of a receipt into an editable expense draft
- **Quick Log** records purchases that have no receipt in a few taps
- **Household categories** such as groceries, food, utilities, transportation, health, and education
- **Budget comparison** shows how actual spending measures up to the household budget

Under the hood, receipt photos are cleaned up (rotation, contrast, sharpening) before text recognition, using OCR.space with a local Tesseract fallback if the online service is unavailable. Receipts are stored privately, and every user signs in with their own account.

**My contribution:** Full-stack developer. I worked across the React front end, the FastAPI back end, the Supabase database, and the receipt-scanning pipeline.

`React 19` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `Vite` `Python` `FastAPI` `Supabase` `OCR.space` `Tesseract` `pytest`

### 🌾 Silo: Surplus Harvest Rescue Platform
[Repository](https://github.com/ritztan/Siloo)

Farms often have produce they can't sell, and it spoils before it reaches people who need it. Silo is a web system that moves that surplus to food banks, community kitchens, pantries, and partner NGOs while it is still fresh.

- **Farmers** post the surplus produce they can't sell
- **Coordinators** match each listing to a recipient organization and schedule its pickup
- **Impact tracking** keeps a record of every kilogram of food rescued

**My contribution:** Full-stack developer. I worked across the React front end, the FastAPI back end, and the Supabase database that tracks listings, matches, pickups, and rescued volumes.

`React 19` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `Vite` `Python` `FastAPI` `Supabase`

### 🏘️ Barangay EMS: Evacuation Management &amp; Resource Allocation System
[Repository](https://github.com/Cedric-AL/Barangay-EMS) · *Team project*

During disasters, barangay officials have to track evacuees, evacuation centers, and relief supplies at the same time, often on paper. Barangay EMS is a desktop system that keeps all three in one place and helps officials decide where supplies should go.

- Evacuation center management with capacity tracking, facility checklists, and archiving
- Evacuee registration and transfers that check a center's space and facilities against each person's needs
- Resource inventory with an allocation planner that calculates required supplies, suggested transfers, and unmet needs from configurable supply rules
- Role-based access: barangay officials approve staff registrations and manage centers; center staff handle evacuees
- Dashboard and reports, including supply shortage reports

**My contribution:** Full-stack developer. I worked across the Java Swing interface and the Supabase database, authentication, and server-side functions.

`Java` `Swing` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL` `Maven`
